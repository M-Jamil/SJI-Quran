SJI Tajweed Quran — Privacy Policy

App name: SJI Tajweed Quran — Tajweed & Translation  |  Developer / legal entity: SJI  |  Package ID: com.sji.sjiquran
Effective date: 21 July 2025  |  Last updated: 5 Oct 2026

Plain-language summary: SJI Tajweed Quran does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline. All content (Quran text, translations, bookmarks, notes, and settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to any server.

1. Who We Are

SJI Tajweed Quran (com.sji.sjiquran) is developed and published by SJI Tech ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the SJI Tajweed Quran application handles information when you use it on Android, iOS, macOS, or Windows.

By installing or using SJI Tajweed Quran, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

SJI Tajweed Quran is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, Android ID)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to external servers
  • Reading history, bookmarks, or notes (these remain only on your device)
  • Audio recordings (used solely for voice notes stored locally)
  • Contact lists, calendar, or camera data

There are no user accounts and no registration process. SJI Tajweed Quran requires no sign-in of any kind.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is created and stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted anywhere:

Data type Purpose Storage location
Bookmarks Save favourite verses On-device SQLite database
Personal notes (text) Annotate verses On-device SQLite database
Voice notes (audio) Record spoken annotations On-device file storage
Last-read position Resume reading where you left off On-device shared preferences
App settings Font size, theme, translation language On-device shared preferences
Downloaded audio recitations Offline playback On-device file storage

Uninstalling SJI Tajweed Quran permanently removes all locally stored data.

4. Internet Access and Network Requests

The core functionality of SJI Tajweed Quran — reading the Quran, viewing translations, bookmarks, and notes — is fully offline. The complete Quran database (114 surahs, 6,236 verses, 14 translations) is bundled inside the app.

The app may use the internet only for the optional download of audio recitation files. These requests go directly to the recitation content server. No personal data or identifiers are sent as part of these requests.

No third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or social media SDKs are included in the app.

5. Device Permissions

SJI Tajweed Quran requests only the permissions necessary for its stated features:

Permission Why it is needed Optional?
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) Record voice notes attached to verses Yes — only requested if you use voice notes
Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) Determine Qibla compass direction Yes — only requested if you open the Qibla feature
Internet (INTERNET) Download optional audio recitation files Yes — core reading works without internet
Storage (Android < 10) Save downloaded audio files Yes — only on older Android versions

You may revoke any permission at any time through your device settings without affecting core reading functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

SJI Tajweed Quran does not integrate any of the following:

  • Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or similar tracking services
  • Advertising networks (AdMob, Facebook Audience Network, etc.)
  • Crash reporting services that transmit data externally
  • Social media SDKs
  • Payment processors

The only external service potentially involved is the Google Play Store platform itself, which is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

SJI Tajweed Quran does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Because we collect no data at all, the app is safe for users of all ages. The app is rated Everyone on Google Play and 4+ on the App Store.

8. Data Security

Since SJI Tajweed Quran does not transmit personal data to any external server, there is no risk of your data being exposed through a server-side breach. All data remains on your device and is subject to the security protections provided by your operating system (Android sandboxing, iOS data protection, etc.).

9. Your Rights

Because SJI Tajweed Quran does not collect or store any personal data on our systems, there is no personal data held by SJI for you to access, correct, export, or delete. All locally stored data (bookmarks, notes, settings) is entirely under your control and can be removed by uninstalling the application.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a policy update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any changes.
